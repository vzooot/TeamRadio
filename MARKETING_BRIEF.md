## What the app is

Team Radio is an **unofficial fan companion app for the top open-wheel motorsport championship**. It's built for race weekends: it tells you when the next session starts, shows the weekend schedule in your own time zone, gives you the results and standings, and gives fans a place to talk while the race is on.

The look is a racing cockpit: dark carbon fibre, neon-edged glass tiles, an LED start-light board and a glowing helmet logo. It looks and feels premium, not like a data table.

## Every feature

### Countdown (home tab)
- Live countdown to **every session** of the next race weekend: practice, sprint qualifying, sprint, qualifying and race. Tap a session chip to count down to it.
- **LED start-light gantry**: five lights that come on one per day as race week approaches (1 light at four days out, all 5 on race day), with "RACE WEEK", "LIGHTS OUT TOMORROW" and "RACE DAY" labels. Lights go out when the race starts.
- The LED board cycles on its own through the session time, date, days to go and round number, with a sci-fi scramble effect.
- Neon countdown tiles (days, hours, minutes and seconds, with the seconds tile glowing orange).
- **"Live now" banner** with a broadcast-style session clock, down to milliseconds, while a session is on track.
- **Race weekend timetable** in the user's local time zone, with a **weather forecast and rain chance per session** and a mini countdown to each one.
- **Session alerts**: an optional notification 15 minutes before every session.
- **Browse any round** of the season from the season strip; the countdown, schedule and circuit follow.

### Live Activity and widgets
- **Pin a session to the Lock Screen**: a Live Activity with a ticking countdown on the Lock Screen and in the **Dynamic Island**, which flips to LIVE at green light.
- **Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets** ("Next Race Session"), in small, medium, inline, circular and rectangular sizes, with self-updating countdowns.

### The circuit
- **Interactive 3D circuit map** built from real track geometry: rotate, zoom and explore.
- Track stats: length, number of corners, direction, pit-lane time loss and elevation.
- Switch to **Apple Maps 3D** of the real venue.

### Results
- Race, sprint and qualifying classification for every round, including the weekend in progress.
- **Podium** view, **fastest lap**, and places gained or lost from the grid.
- **Spoiler mode**: results stay hidden until you tap to reveal, for people watching later.
- Constructors' championship on the same tab.

### Standings
- Full drivers' and constructors' championships with points, wins and the gap to the leader.

### Paddock (community)
- **One live chat room per race weekend.** No sign-up: it uses the user's iCloud account and a unique paddock name.
- **Private messages, end-to-end encrypted**, with photos, videos and GIFs.
- Push notifications for the room (can be muted) and for private messages, message sounds, and app icon badges.
- Moderation: report and block, and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

### News
- Latest stories from Formula1.com, BBC Sport and Motorsport.com in one feed. Stories open at the original source.

### Coming in the next update (NOT live yet — don't promote until the owner says it has shipped)
- **Driver and team profile cards**: tap any driver or team for season stats, career firsts, a race-by-race strip and team base, with original, generic artwork in team colours (cars and race suits), not real photos.
- **Invite banner** ("Call in your crew") on the home tab, which opens the share sheet with the App Store link.

## Who it's for

1. **The race-weekend regular:** follows every Grand Prix, watches every session, and wants the schedule in their own time zone plus results the moment the flag drops.
2. **The time-zone sufferer:** races at 06:00 or 23:00 local time; they need reliable local times, reminders and a Lock Screen countdown.
3. **The delayed viewer:** watches on replay and needs spoiler mode.
4. **The social fan:** wants to talk with other fans during the race without joining Discord or Reddit threads.
5. **The design lover:** chooses apps because they look great (dark mode, neon, widgets, Dynamic Island).

Markets: English-speaking first; Sweden and the Nordics are a strong second (full Swedish localisation).

## What makes it special

- **Built for race weekends**, not a general sports app.
- **Cockpit design**: LED start lights that count down the days, neon tiles and a 3D circuit. It looks like nothing else in the category.
- **Live Activity and Dynamic Island countdown** that flips to LIVE at green light.
- **A chat room for every race weekend, with no sign-up**, plus encrypted private messages.
- **Spoiler mode** for replay watchers.
- **Free, no ads, no tracking, no account**, and open source.

## Rules every agent must follow

### Approval: nothing goes public without the owner
- **Never post, publish, reply, message, comment, upvote, or contact anyone.** Agents produce drafts, plans and analysis only.
- Anything meant to go public (posts, replies, listing changes, outreach) goes to the owner as a **decision**. The owner publishes it himself.
- **Never spend money**, sign up for paid tools or start trials without explicit approval.
- Only work inside your Paperclip project folder. Never read or change anything else on the computer.

### Legal: stay clearly unofficial
- Team Radio is an **independent, unofficial fan app**. Never say, suggest or imply that it is official, licensed, endorsed by or affiliated with any league, series, governing body, team or driver.
- **No official logos, trademarks or branding.** No team or driver photos or likenesses, and no official broadcast or TV programme names. Use the app's own screenshots and artwork only.
- **History:** the app was once rejected by App Store review under **guideline 4.1(a)** for resembling official league content. App Store text especially must stay clearly independent.
- Factual references are fine, such as race and circuit names, drivers mentioned in results, or "the next race weekend". The in-app disclaimer covers the data. Never use them in a way that suggests affiliation.
- Follow each community's own rules. Many motorsport subreddits restrict self-promotion; check before drafting, and say so in the draft.

### Tone
- A fan talking to fans: enthusiastic, specific and short. No hype words, no fake reviews, no invented download or user numbers.
- Lead with what the user gets (never miss a session, results without spoilers, a Lock Screen countdown), not with the technology.
