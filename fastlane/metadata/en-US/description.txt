Team Radio is a fast, beautiful companion app for motorsport race weekends, and it's made for iPhone Duo.

MADE FOR IPHONE DUO
• Unfold it and Team Radio becomes two screens: a display (the race and its countdown, the 3D circuit or the standings) and, beside it, the screen where you pick what it shows
• Sideways like a book or propped up like a laptop, the screens follow the fold and nothing sits on it
• Results, News, Games and the Paddock work the same way: pick on one screen, see it on the other
• On iPad held sideways, the same two screens

COUNTDOWN
• Live countdown to every session of the upcoming race weekend — practice, qualifying, sprint, and race
• Start-light gantry that fills up as race week approaches
• Full weekend timetable in your local timezone with per-session weather forecasts
• Broadcast-style session clock with milliseconds while a session is live

THE CIRCUIT IN 3D
• Real track shapes rendered in interactive 3D — rotate, zoom, explore
• Corner markers, track length, direction, and pit-lane time loss

RESULTS & STANDINGS
• Podium and full classification for every completed round of the season, including sprints
• Qualifying results with session times and optional spoiler mode
• Complete drivers' and teams' championship standings

ALWAYS ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
• Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets with self-updating countdowns
• An automatic Live Activity before every session: a ticking countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island that flips to LIVE at green light (you choose how early)
• Optional alerts 15 minutes before every session

PADDOCK CHAT
• A live fan chat room for every race weekend — no account or sign-up needed
• Unique paddock names, with reporting and blocking built in

NEWS
• The latest racing stories from major motorsport outlets, in one feed

Team Radio is free, collects no data, and requires no account.

Team Radio is an independent fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any motorsport league, governing body, series organizer, or team. All race data comes from community-maintained public sources.
