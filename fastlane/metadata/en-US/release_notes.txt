Made for iPhone Duo

Unfold it and Team Radio becomes two screens:
• The race on one: the countdown as a glowing ring clock, the start lights, Ask Emily and the season
• The weekend on the other: the 3D circuit, the schedule with the weather, and the standings
• Hold it sideways like a book or prop it up like a laptop: the two screens follow the fold, and nothing sits on it
• Results sit beside the standings after each round, Games put your picks next to the arcade, and News opens a story beside the list
• Lights Out and Reaction Wall play with their board beside the game

Also new:
• Settings behind the steering wheel: an automatic Lock Screen countdown before every session (1, 2, 4 or 8 hours ahead) and a switch for every kind of notification
• A champion moment when the title is decided, and a crown over the standings
• Fixes for a Lock Screen countdown crash and two rare crashes
